# Northstar Commerce — Refund and Return Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the eligibility requirements for product returns and refunds.

## 2. Change-of-mind returns

Customers may request a change-of-mind return within 30 calendar days of delivery.

Products must be returned with their original packaging, included accessories and proof of purchase.

Products showing damage caused by customer misuse may be ineligible for a change-of-mind return.

## 3. Damaged or defective products

Customers who receive damaged or defective products may request a refund or replacement.

Support agents must verify the original order and obtain sufficient information about the reported issue.

Claims involving defective products must not be automatically rejected solely because the standard change-of-mind return window has expired.

## 4. Refund amount

Refunds must not exceed the eligible amount originally paid for the affected products.

All refund amounts must be calculated using verified order and payment information.

## 5. Refund processing

Approved refunds should be returned to the original payment method where possible.

Refund execution must be performed through the authorised payment system.

## 6. Approval requirements

Refunds requiring management approval must follow the Refund Approval Policy before execution.
