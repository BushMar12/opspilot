# Northstar Commerce — Refund Approval Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes approval requirements for financial actions performed by customer support staff.

## 2. Standard refunds

Refund requests of AUD 500 or less may follow the standard refund workflow, provided all eligibility requirements are satisfied and the staff member has the necessary authority.

## 3. High-value refunds

Refund requests exceeding AUD 500 require management approval before execution.

The approval request must include the customer identifier, order identifier, proposed refund amount and supporting justification.

## 4. Exceptional refunds

Refund requests involving suspected fraud, unusual payment activity or policy exceptions must be escalated for manual review.

This requirement applies regardless of the refund amount.

## 5. Approval restrictions

Support agents must not approve their own high-value refund requests.

A pending approval does not authorise refund execution.

Rejected or cancelled requests must not be executed.

## 6. Audit requirements

All refund recommendations, approval decisions and execution outcomes must be recorded in the audit trail.
